1 · SHENZHEN · SUMMIT

XI GREETS TRUMP ON THE TARMAC IN SHENZHEN

Xi Jinping personally greets Donald Trump on the tarmac at Shenzhen Baoan airport — a handshake and hug at the foot of Air Force One, believed to be the first time Xi has personally greeted an arriving US president — as Trump lands for APEC. Xi meets Vladimir Putin in the city the same day; Trump and Putin shake hands, their first face-to-face encounter since the August 2025 Alaska summit. Xi delivers a keynote at the APEC CEO Summit and hosts a welcome banquet in the evening. The trilateral summit is Thursday 19 Nov. [Le Monde]

IRAN HITS KUWAIT AND BAHRAIN AFTER US STRIKE ON QESHM

Iran struck Kuwait and Bahrain after the US hit a missile site on Qeshm Island. Kuwait engaged drones after Iran said it targeted Ali Al Salem Air Base, and Bahrain reported intercepting several attacks. [WSJ]

SAUDI COALITION HITS 60 HOUTHI TARGETS

Saudi-led coalition launches major airstrikes against Houthi targets in Yemen on Wednesday — the coalition says it struck 60 Houthi targets — after Iran's Gulf strikes; Houthis answer with missiles toward Riyadh and the coalition says it intercepted a missile heading for Riyadh; Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE condemn the Iranian attacks on Kuwait and Bahrain. Nvidia halts sales of its US-approved H200 chips to China on Wednesday and creates a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee; interim CEO Colette Kress says Nvidia sold $2.4bn of Hopper-line chips in China in Q3 but will no longer sell them there; shares fall despite the bumper quarter.
